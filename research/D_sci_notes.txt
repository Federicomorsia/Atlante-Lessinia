Aggregates from the same sources:
- Lessinia trentina 2013–2025: 136 events, 143 dead (132 cattle), 31 injured (ilDolomiti, 5 Sep 2025).
- PAT-cited thesis 2012–2024: 711 events, 952 animals, 499 attacks on alpeggi involving 574 cattle, 99 alpeggi with at least one predation, 13 alpeggi with 10 or more events (about half of pasture cattle predations), 4 clusters in 2022–24 (ilDolomiti, 3 May 2026).
- Tabarelli: 94 malghe mapped (82 VR, 12 TN), p. 22.

Main contradictions (also in the notes field):
- Pair confirmation: Menini dates it to paired tracks on 12 Apr 2012. Tabarelli dates it to genetic samples at Malga Revoltel on 30 Oct 2012.
- Veronese predations 2012–15: DGR 1080 and Vareschi give different figures. DGR 1080 counts wolf and dog together.
- 2024 pack status: Tabarelli/Parricelli and the association "Io non ho paura del lupo" disagree on which pack bred and with how many pups.
- 2025 removal date: the PAT press release puts it on the night before 20 Sep; the TED report gives 19 Sep.
